Add trimmed-mean/median inflation and a CPI->PCE bridge

Two new models behind the existing Model bar, built to the same contract as
the ISM index and the supply/demand decomposition: a Python engine whose
functions map to numbered equations, a parity-tested browser twin, pinned
config, and validation against published ground truth.

Trimmed mean & median (Eqs. T1-T4, src/ism/trim_engine.py)
  Weighted median and asymmetric trimmed means over the category
  cross-section, with partial inclusion at the trim points so the estimator
  stays continuous in the trim fractions. Three scopes: the Cleveland Fed's
  45-component cut (owners' equivalent rent split by census region), this
  repo's 70 CPI item strata, and BEA PCE underlying detail.

  vs the published series, 1998-:
    Cleveland median CPI      12m corr 0.997  RMSE 0.083  bias  0.000
    Cleveland 16% trimmed CPI 12m corr 0.998  RMSE 0.114  bias +0.084
    Dallas trimmed mean PCE   12m corr 0.990  RMSE 0.127  bias +0.063

  Three things measured rather than assumed: BEA's 2.4.4U price indexes
  already arrive seasonally adjusted (re-adjusting costs 0.9970 -> 0.9951);
  BLS publishes no CUSR series for every stratum and where it does not the
  NSA index is the adjusted one (7 of 70); splitting OER by region takes the
  median replication from 0.965 to 0.997.

Versioned CPI weights (src/ism/cpi_ri.py)
  December relative importances 1997-2025, price-updated month by month.
  Within a weight regime this is BLS's own method, and it checks out: in the
  non-update years of the biennial regime the price-updated and published
  December weights agree to ~0.02pp summed across seventy strata. Pre-1998
  months are back-updated from the 1997 anchor and labelled as such.

CPI -> PCE (Eqs. C1-C6, src/ism/cpi_pce.py)
  A six-term telescoping identity for the CPI-PCE wedge - rebuild residual,
  coverage, weight, price-measure, scope, formula - closing to 4e-16, plus a
  group-level nowcast bridge that never sees the month it predicts (monthly
  RMSE 0.121pp). The fitted pass-through slopes are the useful output:
  shelter 1.00, medical services 0.31.

New data sources, all keyless: the BLS flat files (which carry the
seasonally adjusted CUSR series and the chained CPI), the BLS relative-
importance archives, and the Cleveland and Dallas Feds' own published files.
download.bls.gov 403s the default python-requests agent; the repo's
USER_AGENT is accepted, and that trap is documented on the client.
